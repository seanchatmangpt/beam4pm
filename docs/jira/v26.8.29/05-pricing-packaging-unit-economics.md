# Pricing, Packaging and Unit Economics — v26.8.29

## Objective

Price beam4pm_pro against the value of process intelligence and governed operational improvement while remaining compatible with AWS, Microsoft and Google marketplace transaction models.

## Packaging principles

1. Make first purchase simple enough for marketplace procurement.
2. Avoid pricing that discourages telemetry or instrumentation.
3. Preserve a clear expansion path across estate size and capability depth.
4. Make air-gap/sovereign operation an explicit premium operating tier.
5. Keep public list pricing defensible while expecting enterprise private offers to carry negotiated TCV.
6. Never create marketplace-specific feature forks when an entitlement policy can express the difference.

## Commercial dimensions to experiment with

Use DfCM: preserve multiple lawful models until customer and marketplace evidence selects the best fit.

### Model A — estate bands

Charge by governed environment/cluster/service-estate band. Advantages: predictable procurement and expansion alignment.

### Model B — process scope

Charge by discovered/governed process-estate band. Advantages: price maps closer to product value; requires a defensible canonical measurement.

### Model C — capacity units

Use marketplace-compatible units representing a basket of estate/process capacity. Advantages: portable across providers; requires excellent customer explanation.

### Model D — platform commitment + overage

Annual/multi-year base commitment with included capacity and metered expansion. Strong fit for private offers and enterprise growth.

Do not choose permanently before observed willingness-to-pay, marketplace constraints, gross-margin analysis and sales-cycle evidence exist.

## Initial pricing hypothesis

The commercial motion should bias toward annual contracts rather than low-value self-serve telemetry billing. A useful initial segmentation for testing is:

- departmental/land: bounded estate, one use case, standard support;
- enterprise: multi-environment, richer inference/governance, private offer;
- sovereign: disconnected/regulated deployment, premium support and lifecycle artifacts.

Actual dollar levels remain `UNKNOWN` until buyer discovery and competitive/willingness-to-pay evidence are collected. Do not turn an arbitrary price guess into doctrine.

## ROI model

beam4pm_pro should quantify value using before/after or avoided-cost evidence.

Potential value terms:

`V = cycle-time reduction + waiting/WIP reduction + rework reduction + incident/retry reduction + labor coordination reduction + throughput gain + avoided tool/consulting cost + risk reduction`

Every term must record source and confidence; do not double-count overlapping benefits.

## Product-measurable economic evidence

Where lawful and observable, collect locally:

- process cycle time;
- waiting time;
- WIP;
- throughput;
- retry/rework loops;
- failed variants;
- conformance deviations;
- mean recovery time for observed process obligations;
- process coverage;
- number of high-confidence optimization candidates;
- realized before/after outcomes after admitted change.

Produce a customer-controlled value receipt suitable for QBR/renewal without requiring raw telemetry export.

## Unit economics

Track separately for SaaS-connected, customer-operated and air-gapped deployments:

- marketplace fees/revenue share;
- infrastructure COGS paid by vendor;
- support labor;
- update/release operations;
- security/compliance cost allocation;
- customer success cost;
- sales/channel commissions;
- partner/reseller margin;
- cloud credits/commitment effects where applicable.

Key formulas:

`Gross Margin = (Revenue - direct COGS) / Revenue`

`CAC Payback = CAC / monthly gross profit from new customer`

`NRR = (starting ARR + expansion - contraction - churn) / starting ARR`

`Value Capture = beam4pm_pro annual cost / independently evidenced annual customer value`

## Marketplace pricing compatibility

### AWS

Support contract dimensions and, where justified, usage beyond contract. Private offers can customize pricing/payment terms. Do not create overlapping metering paths that can double bill.

### Microsoft

Support SaaS plans with metered billing where selected and private-plan/offer enterprise negotiation. Explicitly map each meter to entitlement and invoicing semantics.

### Google

Support subscription, usage or hybrid private-offer structures where product form permits. Preserve entitlement/order identity for multiple-order scenarios.

## Discount governance

No arbitrary discounting. Private-offer discounts require a reason code such as:

- multi-year commitment;
- estate volume;
- strategic lighthouse/reference rights;
- reseller/channel economics;
- migration/competitive displacement;
- prepaid terms;
- early design-partner risk.

Track list price, net price, discount, marketplace/provider fees and partner margin separately.

## Expansion model

Revenue can expand on two orthogonal axes:

`Revenue Opportunity ≈ estate coverage × capability depth`

Estate expansion: cluster -> account/subscription/project -> business unit -> enterprise -> multi-cloud.

Capability expansion: Observe -> Infer -> Govern -> Sovereign/Actuation-oriented controls.

## Renewal test

A renewal is defensible when at least one of these is receipted:

- sustained process intelligence usage;
- measurable operational/economic gain;
- critical compliance/governance dependence;
- expanded process/estate coverage;
- validated avoided cost/risk;
- customer-designated strategic platform status.

If renewal relies only on sunk integration cost, product standing is weak and must trigger RCA.

# GTM, ICP and Sales Playbook — v26.8.29

## Commercial thesis

beam4pm_pro should enter accounts as an observation/inference product, prove process value with minimal initial write authority, then expand into deeper governance, planning and estate coverage.

The sale is not initially “replace your process stack.” It is:

> Show us your living systems and we will reconstruct the processes they actually execute, locally, with evidence.

## Ideal customer profile

Prioritize organizations with several of these properties:

- large distributed service estates;
- Kubernetes and OpenTelemetry adoption or strong observability data;
- high process complexity across services/queues/jobs/databases;
- regulated or sensitive data boundaries;
- expensive incidents/retries/rework;
- process-excellence / operational-excellence programs;
- SRE/platform engineering plus business-process ownership gaps;
- substantial cloud commitments that make marketplace procurement attractive;
- air-gap/sovereign requirements underserved by SaaS-only vendors;
- material cost of consultants/manual process discovery.

Vertical hypotheses:

- financial services;
- healthcare/life sciences;
- defense/government/critical infrastructure;
- manufacturing/logistics;
- telecom;
- large SaaS/platform companies;
- transportation;
- insurance/claims;
- retail/fulfillment.

These are hypotheses until pipeline evidence selects them.

## Buyer map

### Economic buyer

CIO, CTO, COO, EVP/SVP operations, transformation executive, platform executive or business-unit leader depending on use case.

### Technical champion

Platform engineering, SRE, observability, enterprise architecture, process intelligence/mining, distributed systems or BEAM engineering.

### Operational champion

Business process owner, Lean/TPS/continuous-improvement leader, service operations, fulfillment, claims, manufacturing operations or equivalent.

### Required approvers

Security, privacy, procurement, cloud/FinOps, legal and architecture review.

## Qualification

Do not qualify merely on technical interest. Require a candidate mission problem.

Discovery questions:

1. Which business outcome crosses the most systems/teams?
2. How do you reconstruct one failed customer/process instance today?
3. How long does that reconstruction take?
4. Which process metrics can you measure reliably now?
5. How many variants do you believe exist, and how is that known?
6. What telemetry/runtime surfaces already exist?
7. What data may not leave the environment?
8. What cloud marketplace/commitment route is preferred?
9. What finding would make a proof economically meaningful?
10. Who owns the process and who owns the runtime?

## Proof-of-value motion

### Entry condition

- bounded environment;
- read-only/observation authority defined;
- target process/problem named;
- data boundary admitted;
- objective evidence criterion agreed.

### Proof outcome

At least one of:

- previously unknown process variant;
- quantified wait/WIP bottleneck;
- repeated retry/recovery loop;
- conformance deviation;
- cross-service/process causal path previously expensive to reconstruct;
- quantified improvement opportunity;
- evidence that an assumed process model is wrong.

### Proof receipt

Record:

- exact deployed beam4pm_pro identity;
- environment scope;
- access granted;
- observations admitted;
- inferred model and confidence;
- finding;
- economic/operational interpretation;
- known unknowns;
- replay procedure.

## Challenger-style commercial narrative

### Reframe

The customer probably has observability but not process observability. Service health does not equal process health.

### Economic consequence

When process state is fragmented, humans become middleware: incidents, escalations, reconciliation, meetings and analysis carry the integration burden.

### New way

Use living-system evidence to infer the process continuously, then let formal models reason over the resulting state space.

### Low-risk first step

Deploy locally with observation authority only. No initial autonomous production changes are required.

## Competitive frame

Do not claim universal superiority without executed comparison. Position the architectural differentiators:

- local/air-gapped-first possibility;
- telemetry-to-process inference;
- living process twin rather than only historical mining;
- BEAM-native semantics plus cloud/runtime integration;
- formal planning/model projections;
- explicit authority/receipt boundary;
- ggen-manufactured implementation and reproducibility.

Competitive proof requires identical subject/boundary/workload comparisons.

## Marketplace sales motion

For every qualified enterprise opportunity, determine early:

- preferred marketplace;
- buyer billing/account/tenant identifiers;
- committed-spend considerations;
- public vs private offer/plan;
- reseller/channel requirement;
- contract term;
- legal/EULA requirements;
- target acceptance date.

Do not leave marketplace routing until legal/procurement after technical selection; it is part of solution architecture.

## Expansion playbook

### Horizontal estate expansion

Start with one process/environment and identify adjacent uncovered execution edges.

### Vertical capability expansion

Move from observe/infer to conformance, formal planning, governance and admitted actuation only when evidence supports value and customer authority exists.

### Sovereign expansion

Where policy prevents SaaS observability/process products, use local/air-gapped capabilities as the wedge rather than treating the environment as unsupported.

## Customer success / QBR

Quarterly evidence should include:

- estate/process coverage;
- discovered/inferred process counts and confidence bands;
- top process variants;
- high-value findings;
- resolved bottlenecks/rework;
- before/after metrics where changes occurred;
- UNKNOWN areas and instrumentation opportunities;
- support record;
- entitlement/utilization;
- next expansion candidates.

## Reference program

A lighthouse customer becomes referenceable only with explicit permission. Capture a reusable evidence structure without exposing customer telemetry:

`problem -> bounded environment -> beam4pm evidence -> finding -> action -> measured result`

## Sales standing

- `QUALIFIED` requires a real process problem, owner and proof criterion.
- `VALUE_PROVEN` requires observed customer-specific evidence.
- `COMMERCIAL_PROPOSED` requires a purchasable package and procurement route.
- `CONTRACTED` requires marketplace/agreement evidence, not verbal intent.
- `DEPLOYED` requires exact environment execution.
- `EXPANDING` requires identified additional estate/capability with customer sponsorship.
- `RENEWED` requires executed renewal agreement/entitlement.
